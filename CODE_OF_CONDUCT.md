# Community Participation Guidelines

This repository is governed by Mozilla's code of conduct and etiquette
guidelines. For more details, please read the
[Mozilla Community Participation Guidelines](https://www.mozilla.org/about/governance/policies/participation/).

## Why these guidelines

The CPG was chosen deliberately over the alternatives. It names the
characteristics it protects rather than gesturing at them, and it draws
directly on the [LGBTQ in Technology Code of Conduct](https://lgbtq.technology/coc.html)
and the WisCon code of conduct.

Everyone is welcome to participate regardless of, among other things:

> Background · Family status · Gender · **Gender identity or expression** ·
> Marital status · **Sex** · **Sexual orientation** · Native language · Age ·
> Ability · Race and/or ethnicity · Caste · National origin ·
> Socioeconomic status · Religion · Geographic location · Any other dimension
> of diversity

Being welcoming is the baseline, not the goal. The CPG expects participants
to behave in ways that actively make space for others, and treats
"unwelcoming behaviour" as a violation whether or not it was intended as one.

## How to report

To report a violation of these guidelines, read Mozilla's
'[How to Report](https://www.mozilla.org/about/governance/policies/participation/reporting/)'
page.

For this project specifically, you can also report directly and privately to
the maintainer: **jordan@evattlabs.com**.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will not be asked to justify why
something made you uncomfortable, and you will not be put in contact with the
person you are reporting.
